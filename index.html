The index is the percentage change in seasonally adjusted job postings relative to Feb 1 2020, using a seven-day trailing average — a reading of 112 means postings are 12% above the pre-pandemic baseline. Indeed seasonally adjusts every series separately (Bundesbank daily-series method, adopted Nov 2024); history is revised on each refresh. Sectors are Indeed's own categorisation of normalised job titles. Only sectors Indeed still publishes appear here — discontinued series drop out automatically whenever the data is rebuilt.
